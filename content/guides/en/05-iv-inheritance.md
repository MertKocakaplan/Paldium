# IV inheritance

Every Pal has three IVs (talents): **HP**, **Attack** and **Defense**, each from 0 to 100.

## How the child gets them
First the game rolls how many stats come from the parents:

| Group | Chance | Stats taken from the parents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 % | one of HP, Attack, Defense (equal chance) |
| 2 | 33 % | all three, or HP+Defense, or HP+Attack (equal chance) |
| 3 | 17 % | HP, Attack and Defense |

For every stat taken, the value comes from either parent with 50 % each. The stats not taken are random between 0 and 100.

## Tips
- Parents with 100 in the stat you care about matter most: a stat is only inherited with a 50 % pick between the parents.
- It is rare to inherit all three, so IV farming takes many generations.
- Mushroom Cake and Extravagant Vegetable Cake add +1 to +5 to each IV, capped at 100.
- Mutants always have IVs between 90 and 100.
