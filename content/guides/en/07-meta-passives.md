# Meta passive sets (game 1.0.5, November 2026)

Community guides disagree on details and change with every patch. This page lists the sets the app offers, with the **values taken from the game data of this app** (version 1.0.5), not from the guides. Treat it as a starting point; the sources are at the end.

## Where passives come from
- **Wild Pals:** only passives that can appear randomly (for example [[passive:CraftSpeed_up3]], [[passive:MoveSpeed_up_3]], [[passive:Deffence_up3]]).
- **Not on wild Pals:** [[passive:Legend]], [[passive:Rare]] (Lucky), the +30 element passives (the "emperors" and "lords"), the dual-element passives and a few others. They are inherited by breeding or come with certain species.
- **Species that carry a passive from birth:** [[passive:Legend]] is guaranteed on [[pal:SaintCentaur]], [[pal:BlackCentaur]], [[pal:IceHorse]], [[pal:IceHorse_Dark]], [[pal:PoseidonOrca]] and [[pal:JetDragon]]. Breed one of them with a Pal that has your other wanted passives; the Planner treats the owned Pal as the start parent.
- **Mutation passives** (for example [[passive:MutationPal_Immortal]], [[passive:MutationPal_Babysitter]]) come only from mutated eggs: about 1 % of eggs, 3 % with the strongest cake (see the mutation guide). Once you own one it can be bred onward like any other passive.
- **World Tree passives** (for example [[passive:WorldTree_CraftSpeed]], [[passive:WorldTree_MoveSpeed]]) come from the World Tree area. They can be bred onward as well.
- The surgery table can replace some passives, but it is expensive and the app's plans never use it.

## Role sets in the app
In the Planner, under "Passives", every role has two sets: **Best** (the fixed set below) and **Easy to get** (computed from passives that can appear on wild Pals). Choosing one fills your four wanted passives; below the passives the app shows where each one comes from and how many of your Pals already have it.

- The breeding farm set is the one that helps this app's own users most: [[passive:Test_PalEgg_HatchingSpeed_Up]] raises breeding speed, [[passive:MutationPal_Babysitter]] hatching speed.

## How to carry Legend and Lucky
[[passive:Legend]] and [[passive:Rare]] never appear on wild Pals, so a plan cannot "find" them. Own a Pal that has them (a guaranteed species above, or a bred Pal), select it as a **preferred Pal** in the Planner and put the passive among the wanted passives; the Planner then builds the path that keeps the passive and adds the others. Remember that every unwanted passive in the pool lowers the chance (see the passive inheritance guide).

## Best workers per work type
The Paldex has a "Best workers" view: for every work type the first three species of the early game (obtainable up to level 15), mid game (up to level 35) and the whole game, by work level, earliest obtain level and number of other work types. Species that can only be obtained by breeding appear in the last stage. The list is computed from the game data, so it follows the data of this app, not a guide.
