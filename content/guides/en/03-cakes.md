# Cakes

Cakes are items put into the Breeding Farm. Each one changes the outcome in a different way.

| Cake | Effect |
|---|---|
| Cake | Starts the breeding. |
| Mushroom Cake | Adds +1 to +5 to every IV of the child (maximum 100). |
| Vegetable Cake | Produces 2 eggs at once. |
| Extravagant Vegetable Cake | Raises the mutation chance from 1 % to 3 % and adds the IV bonus. |
| Special Cake | Fixes the passive target at 4 and lets the child inherit all active attacks. |

## Special Cake
Normally the number of inherited passives is rolled (1 to 4). With a Special Cake the target is always 4: if the parents hold four or more passives the child gets four of them, otherwise it gets all of them and the free slots are filled randomly. Active skills: instead of one random attack, every inheritable attack is passed on.

## Tips
- Use the Special Cake for the last step of a plan, where 4 passives are needed.
- The Extravagant Vegetable Cake is the way to farm mutations; see the guide on mutation.
- Mushroom and Extravagant Vegetable cakes help with IVs only when the child's inherited IVs are not already 100.
